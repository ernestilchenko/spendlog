Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Spendlog is a private, non-commercial budgeting tool run by one person for their own finances. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data is processed

Transactions, balances and account identifiers (IBAN) of the owner's own bank accounts, read through Enable Banking with the owner's explicit consent given in the bank; payments sent by the owner's iPhone; and data the owner types in by hand.

Why

Only to show the owner's spending, income, budgets and savings goals. The data is not sold, shared, used for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored

On a server owned and operated by the owner in Poland. Backups, when made, stay under the owner's control. Merchant names may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API to suggest a category; no account numbers or balances are sent.

How long

Until the owner deletes it. Bank access can be revoked at any time in the bank or by disconnecting the bank in Spendlog; access also expires automatically after 180 days.

Contact

The owner of this site is the only data subject and controller.
